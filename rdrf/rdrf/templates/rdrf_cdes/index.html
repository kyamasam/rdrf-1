{% trans "About the Kenya Chronic Diseases Registry" %}

{% blocktrans %} The Kenya Clinical Research Health Network (CREA-N) Chronic Diseases Registry is a comprehensive platform designed to track and manage critical health conditions including Cancer, Diabetes, and Hypertension. This registry enables healthcare professionals and policymakers to: - Monitor disease prevalence and trends - Improve patient care and treatment strategies - Support evidence-based health policy decisions - Enhance national health research capabilities {% endblocktrans %}
